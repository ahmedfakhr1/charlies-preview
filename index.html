Our story

Named for Charles Baehler, known as the godfather of Egyptian hospitality.

The house is inspired by an era of elegance and ease. You find it in Korba, Heliopolis, on El Thawra Street, under brass-framed arches indoors and among palms in the garden.

Tables are set from 9 AM every day. Breakfast is served until 1:30 PM, and dinner and desserts follow until 1 AM.

By day

Breakfast, 9 AM to 1:30 PM

Eggs, toasts, bowls and oven dishes, plus Charlie's Feteer and a Royal Breakfast to share.

Dinner and desserts, until 1 AM

Pasta, pizza, burgers and mains, then desserts such as Tiramisu and Baked Ricotta Cheesecake.
