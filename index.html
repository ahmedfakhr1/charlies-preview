Our story

Inspired by an era of elegance and ease.

Charlie's is named for Charles Baehler, known as the godfather of Egyptian hospitality.

In Korba, Heliopolis, the tables are set from 9 AM: breakfast until 1:30 PM, then dinner and desserts until 1 AM, under the brass arches inside or out in the garden.
